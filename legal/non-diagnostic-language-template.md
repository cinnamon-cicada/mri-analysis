# Non-Diagnostic Language Template

Use the phrases below verbatim (or close paraphrases) wherever the app surfaces brain comparison results to users. The goal is to clearly convey statistical meaning while preventing any reasonable interpretation as a medical diagnosis.

---

## API Response Template

```json
{
  "disclaimer": "These results are a statistical comparison only and do not constitute a medical diagnosis or clinical assessment. Consult a licensed healthcare professional for any health concerns.",
  "summary": "Your brain measurements were compared against a reference population of {n} individuals aged {age_range}.",
  "measurements": [
    {
      "region": "Left Hippocampus",
      "your_value_mm3": 3200,
      "population_mean_mm3": 3400,
      "population_std_mm3": 300,
      "percentile": 25,
      "interpretation": "Your left hippocampal volume falls in approximately the 25th percentile of the reference group. This means roughly 75% of individuals in the comparison group had a larger measurement. This is a statistical observation, not a finding of abnormality."
    }
  ],
  "footer": "Brain structure varies widely among healthy individuals. A measurement outside a typical range does not indicate disease. Only a qualified clinician reviewing your full history, symptoms, and imaging in context can make a medical determination."
}
```

## Frontend UI Copy Snippets

### Results Page Header
> Your brain measurements have been compared to a population reference dataset. The percentages and numbers below describe **statistical differences only** — they are not a diagnosis and should not be used as one.

### Per-Region Tooltip
> This value shows how your measurement compares statistically to others in the reference group. Being above or below average is normal and does not indicate a medical condition.

### Prominent Disclaimer Banner (shown above all results)
> **Not medical advice.** These results are generated by an automated research tool. They describe statistical patterns and are intended for informational and research purposes only. If you have questions about your neurological health, please speak with a licensed medical professional.

### "What does this mean?" Expandable Section
> Brain volumes vary considerably from person to person based on genetics, age, sex, and many other factors. A result in the 10th or 90th percentile is **not automatically cause for concern**. Scientists use measurements like these to study populations — not to diagnose individuals. Please do not make medical decisions based solely on this output.

## Rules for Contributors

When writing any result-facing copy for this project:

1. **Never use clinical language** such as "abnormal", "atrophy", "lesion", "pathology", "disease", "disorder", or "deficiency" in user-facing output.
2. **Always frame results as statistical comparisons**, not deviations from a norm.
3. **Always include a disclaimer** on any page or API response that surfaces individual measurements.
4. **Never imply causation** between a brain measurement and a behavioral or cognitive outcome (e.g., do not say "lower hippocampal volume causes memory problems").
5. **When referencing ADHD or other conditions** in educational content, use hedged language: "Research suggests an association between…" not "ADHD causes…".
