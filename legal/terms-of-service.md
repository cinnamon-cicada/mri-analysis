## 2. Description of Service

The Service provides statistical comparison of user-uploaded brain MRI scans against anonymized population-level reference data. Results are descriptive and statistical in nature only.

## 3. NOT Medical Advice

**The Service does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.** All outputs are statistical comparisons intended for research and educational purposes only. No output from this Service should be interpreted as a clinical finding, a diagnosis, or a substitute for professional medical evaluation.

If you have concerns about your neurological health, consult a licensed medical professional.

## 4. Neuroimaging Data — Sensitive Data Handling

You acknowledge that MRI brain scan files may constitute sensitive personal health information. By uploading data, you represent and warrant that:

- You are the data subject (the person whose brain was scanned), **or**
- You have obtained all necessary consents and legal authority to share the data on behalf of the data subject, **or**
- The data has been fully de-identified in accordance with applicable law (e.g., HIPAA Safe Harbor or Expert Determination standards).

We do not knowingly accept identifiable patient data from third parties without a valid data use agreement.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Uploaded scan files are processed and stored temporarily for the purpose of generating your results. Files are automatically deleted from our servers within **30 days** of upload unless you request earlier deletion. Processed statistical outputs (not raw scans) may be retained in anonymized, aggregated form for research purposes.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
